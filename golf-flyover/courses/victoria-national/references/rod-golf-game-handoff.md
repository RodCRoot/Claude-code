## Decision style (observed in the interview — real signal, not guesses)

- **Disciplined when the right miss drowns**: chose 6-iron off the tee on a 323-yd
  hole with water right in the slice zone (VN 2), 7-wood on VN 13 and the closer
  VN 18, 3-wood on the island-fairway VN 17.
- **Aggressive when reward justifies it**: driver at the 311-yd 4th despite water
  both sides; driver on 12 where his fade takes the left water out of play.
- **Trusts elevation**: clubs down on big drops (9-iron on VN 7 and VN 16).
- **Takes the extra club on forced carries**: 7-wood/5-iron on the 176-yd
  all-carry 11th, aiming left-center.

## Standing guidance for any Claude working with Rod on golf

1. His slice right is the #1 leak — when advising a shot, always name where
   "right" ends up before anything else.
2. Respect his aggressive defaults (driver, send-it seconds) — steer with lines
   and targets rather than telling him to club down, except where this plan
   already clubs down (2, 13, 17, 18, and the par 3s as noted).
3. 100 yards is his comfort wedge — when laying up, lay up to 100, not "close."
4. Distances here are plan-level, not laser-level: ±10 yds.
