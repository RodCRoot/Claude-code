# Family Discount

The one discount the bot **is** authorized to offer. Part of [[Customer Service Bot]]; applies to rates in [[Rate Card]].

| Family members with active memberships | Discount, each |
|---|---|
| 2 | 10% |
| 3 | 15% |
| 4+ | 20% |

- **Parents count**, not just siblings.
- Everyone counted must hold an active membership. If one drops, the tier drops for everyone.
- This is the "15% Off" that appears on many ZenPlanner accounts: it is the 3-member tier, not a standing discount. A single member pays list.
- Known defect: when a sibling joins, the existing athlete's rate is often not updated. Check on every new sibling.
