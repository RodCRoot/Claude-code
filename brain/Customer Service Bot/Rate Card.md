# Rate Card

Monthly, billed on the 1st by autopay. Part of [[Customer Service Bot]]. Discounts: [[Family Discount]].

| Level | Month-to-month | 6-month | 12-month |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1x per week | $129.00 | not offered | not offered |
| 2x per week | $232.99 | $197.99 | $178.99 |
| 3x per week | $348.00 | $261.99 | $221.99 |
| 4x per week | $465.00 | $375.99 | not offered |
| Unlimited | $569.00 | $419.99 | $309.99 |

## Selling the commitment
3x/week is $348 month-to-month vs $221.99 on a 12-month: $126/month less, over $1,500/year. Lead with the savings, not the commitment.

## Rules
- No sign-up fee.
- Mid-month starts are **pro-rated**. The bot says so but does **not** do the math; staff handle the amount.
- After any commitment ends it continues month-to-month: [[Cancellation and Commitments]].
- Voice: spell numbers out ("two hundred twenty-two dollars a month").

## ZenPlanner template issues (fix in ZenPlanner, not a bot problem)
- "1x per Week Month to Month" template has Duration: 3 months. Name and term disagree.
- **In Season** template has Auto-Renew ON. This is the cause of the "drifting on $99 for months" pattern. Turn it off at the template.
- College Athlete Weekly also auto-renews (weekly).
