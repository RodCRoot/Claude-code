# Programs and Class Schedule

Part of [[Customer Service Bot]]. Prices live in [[Rate Card]].

## Main program: Sports Performance Training
Coached group sessions on a membership with a monthly session allotment. Sold by weekly frequency (1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, Unlimited) and term (month-to-month, 6-month, 12-month).

## Sessions per month
| Level | Sessions/month |
|---|---|
| 1x per week | 4 |
| 2x per week | 8 |
| 3x per week | 12 |
| 4x per week | 16 (inferred, to confirm) |
| Unlimited | no cap |

Pitch: "A 3x membership is really twelve sessions a month, so a busy week doesn't cost you anything." This is also the basis for [[Make-Up Sessions]].

## Adult training
Same membership structure and pricing, but a **separate approach**: more focused and hands-on, fit to the individual. **Not** the athlete program and **not** one-on-one personal training. For weekend-warrior athletes and people who want to stay strong and keep muscle as they age.

## Other offers
- **In Season** $99/mo, see [[Retention Ladder]]
- **6-week challenge** $299 single payment, 7 weeks, no auto-renew
- **College Athlete Weekly** $50/week, good to offer unprompted to college kids home on break
