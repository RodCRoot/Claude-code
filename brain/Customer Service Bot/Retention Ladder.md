# Retention Ladder

What the bot does when someone wants to pause, hold, or cancel. Part of [[Customer Service Bot]]. Work down; never jump to the bottom.

**Guiding rule (Rod):** if it keeps someone from cancelling or going on hold, it's a good thing. Offer it.

## 1. Offer In Season first
**$99/month.** They keep training, aim for at least once a week, more if they can. Formally: max 2 months per 365 days, 12-month members. **Practically:** offer it to anyone, including members who don't technically qualify, when the alternative is losing them.

Why it beats a hold: a hold stops them entirely; In Season keeps them in the building, progressing, and connected.

> "Before we pause anything, a lot of families do our in-season option. It's ninety-nine a month and they come once a week, or whenever they can fit it in, so they don't lose what they've built. Want me to see about setting that up?"

## 2. Float the come-back commitment
> "And when their season wraps up, would you be interested in finishing out with a twelve-month? You'd keep the twelve-month rate going forward."

Worked example: a 6-month member at month 5 asks for a hold. They don't qualify, offer In Season anyway, then a 12-month at the 12-month rate afterward. Roughly six extra committed months for us, a better rate for them.

## 3. Hold, only if In Season doesn't fit
Max 2 months per membership year, firm restart date, owner-approved (Rod and Erin).

## 4. Cancellation is the last rung
See [[Cancellation and Commitments]].

## Bot limits
- May pitch In Season to anyone and float the 12-month come-back.
- May **not** finalize rates, dates, or eligibility. Staff confirm and build it.
- Never quote how many in-season months remain.
- One good offer, then respect the answer.

## Staff mechanics (not the bot)
- **Never cancel a commitment membership to pause it.** Use Hold on the existing membership with a firm restart; proof is the string `extended by N months on hold` under See More.
- In-season: pause the current membership, add In Season with explicit begin/end, **auto-renew OFF**, set the original to restart, put the dates in writing to the parent.
- Schedule a GHL SMS 2 weeks before restart.
- Template defect: In Season ships with auto-renew ON ([[Rate Card]]).
