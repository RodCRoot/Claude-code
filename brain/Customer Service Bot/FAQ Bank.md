# FAQ Bank

Short, speakable answers. Load these into GHL's FAQ trainer first. Part of [[Customer Service Bot]]. Verbatim extended versions are in `cs-bot/knowledge-base.md`.

**How much does it cost?** It depends on the program that fits, so the easiest way to know is a free assessment. Want me to find you a time? ([[Rate Card]]; don't lead with price.)

**How do I get started?** A free assessment: 45 to 60 minutes. ([[Free Assessment]])

**Is it a tryout / my kid is nervous.** Not at all, nothing to pass or fail. The first part is mostly talking.

**Free trial?** We don't do trials, but we have a 28-day risk-free policy. ([[Cancellation and Commitments]])

**Sibling discount?** Two members is ten percent off each, three is fifteen, four or more is twenty. Parents count. ([[Family Discount]])

**Do you train adults? Is it personal training?** Yes, fit to you, not the athlete program and not one-on-one. ([[Programs and Class Schedule]])

**How many sessions do I get?** Four a month on 1x, eight on 2x, twelve on 3x.

**We missed a month, do we lose sessions?** No: punch card, last three months, membership must be active. ([[Make-Up Sessions]])

**Session is in an hour and the app won't let me sign up.** No problem, I'll get them added. It closes two hours out for coach ratio. ([[Late Reservation and 2-Hour Cutoff]])

**How do I cancel?** Email memberships@teamworkbloomington.com for the 30-day notice; I'll flag it for the team now too.

**What happens when my 6 or 12 months is up? I see another term queued.** You're not locked in; it goes month to month at your rate.

**We need a break for the season.** Offer In Season at ninety-nine a month, once a week. ([[Retention Ladder]])

**My athlete got hurt.** Sympathy, then keep training around it; no doctor's note needed. ([[Injury Handling]])

**My card was declined / how do I update it?** Zen Planner app, profile picture, View Profile, Bills and Payments, Payment Methods. ([[Failed Payment Recovery]])

**I updated my card, am I set?** Thanks! Let me have someone confirm it, then we'll follow up. (Bot never confirms itself.)

**Can I give you my card number?** I can't take card numbers; please enter it in the app.

**When am I billed?** The first of each month by autopay; a mid-month start is pro-rated.

**Fitness Together / Linton?** Mary Hogue's, not ours: (812) 381-1217. ([[Fitness Together and Linton]])
