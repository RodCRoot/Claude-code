# Free Assessment

The conversion hook. Everything the bot does with a lead drives here. Part of [[Customer Service Bot]].

**45-60 minutes. Free. Not a sales pitch.** There are **no trials**; the 28-day policy ([[Cancellation and Commitments]]) is a risk-free guarantee on a real membership.

## Lead with reassurance, unprompted
Kids hear "assessment" and think tryout; parents sense the hesitation and don't book. Say it early: "Tell them not to be nervous. It's not a test, nothing to pass or fail."

## What happens, in order
1. Talk: goals, get to know each other, how we help them level up.
2. Brief movement screen: snapshot of how they move, check nothing is painful (rarely is; if it is, we want to know). **Not a fitness test; they likely won't sweat.**
3. Turf, short warmup.
4. **Force plate jump assessment**: how they apply force into the ground, what they need to train, and a baseline to track progress.
5. Standard vertical jump.
6. Sprints at a few distances: short ones for acceleration/first step, plus a 25-yard for overall speed.
7. Basic strength movements if time allows; otherwise covered in the first session.

## Selling points
- The **force plate is the differentiator**: measured, not guessed.
- They leave with a real baseline and can watch it move.
- Zero pressure.

## Booking
Bot collects athlete name, age/grade, sport, parent name + phone, main goal, then books through the GHL calendar.

## Open
What to wear/bring (default: workout clothes, training shoes, water), waiver timing, and what happens after they say yes: [[Open Questions]].
