Teamwork Bloomington

Member User Agreement: Policies, Procedures, and Fees

Business: T2 Indiana, Inc., doing business as Teamwork Bloomington and Teamwork Indiana ("Teamwork"), 2013 S. Yost Ave, Bloomington, IN. Phone and text 812-445-5551. Email memberships@teamworkbloomington.com.

This User Agreement explains how Teamwork runs day to day. It applies to every member, athlete, and parent or guardian on a Teamwork account. It works together with the Teamwork Membership Agreement and Contract. If the two ever conflict, the Membership Agreement and Contract controls.

In this document, "you" means the adult account holder who signs, and "athlete" means each person training on the account.

1. How Teamwork works

  • Every new athlete starts with a free athletic assessment meeting (about 45 minutes): goals, a movement screen, and athletic testing.
  • Training is coach-led in small groups, with no more than 5 athletes per coach.
  • Each athlete's program is built and tracked in TeamBuildr. Testing data is collected with tools such as force plates, jump testing, and timing gates.
  • A parent or caregiver receives a virtual progress check within the athlete's first month and about every three months after that. These checks cover progress, questions, and next goals.
  • Athletes must be at least 9 years old and able to focus and follow coaching instructions safely for a full session. Coaches make the final call on readiness.

2. Booking sessions

  • ZenPlanner (app or website) is the official schedule. Athletes reserve sessions in ZenPlanner.
  • Each plan comes with a monthly session allowance based on weekly frequency. For example, a 2x per week plan includes 8 sessions per calendar month. Your plan's allowance is shown on your ZenPlanner membership.
  • Reserved spots hold a place another athlete could have used. Please cancel a reservation in ZenPlanner as soon as you know the athlete cannot attend.

3. No-shows

A no-show is a reserved session the athlete does not attend without canceling the reservation in ZenPlanner before the session starts.

  • First no-show: a friendly reminder. No charge.
  • Each no-show after that: a $10 no-show fee, billed to the account.

4. Makeup sessions

  • Unused sessions may be used as makeups for as long as the athlete has an active membership.
  • Makeups depend on open spots in the schedule.
  • Unused sessions have no cash value. They are not refunded, credited, or transferred, and they are forfeited when a membership ends.
  • Not attending does not stop billing. Billing only stops through a hold or cancellation done the way the Membership Agreement describes.

6. Arrival, gear, and the training floor

  • Arrive about 5 minutes early. Late arrivals may need to do a separate warm-up before joining.
  • Wear athletic clothing and clean athletic shoes. Bring a water bottle.
  • Tell a coach before the session about any pain, injury, illness, or doctor's restriction.
  • Use equipment only as coached. Put equipment back after use.
  • Phones stay put away during training unless a coach asks for them.
  • Parents and guests may watch from areas staff designate and should let coaches do the coaching during the session.

7. Conduct

Teamwork is a team environment. Athletes and families are expected to be respectful to coaches, other athletes, and other families. Bullying, harassment, threats, fighting, unsafe horseplay, damaging equipment, or abusive language toward anyone is not allowed. Teamwork may warn, remove from a session, suspend, or end the membership of anyone who does not follow these rules. A membership ended for conduct is treated as a cancellation by the member under the Membership Agreement.

8. Injuries, illness, and concussions

  • Teamwork coaches are trained to modify programming around most injuries and can coordinate with the athlete's health care providers.
  • Concussions, spinal injuries, and other injuries a coach determines are unsafe to train around require written clearance from a licensed health care provider before the athlete returns.
  • If an injury prevents training, email memberships@teamworkbloomington.com to discuss a hold or, with documentation, cancellation. The one-week hold notice is waived for injuries.
  • Athletes who are sick, feverish, or contagious should stay home. Missed sessions can be made up under Section 4.

9. Holidays, weather, and closures

  • Teamwork is closed on major U.S. holidays. Sessions missed for holidays and weather closures can be made up under Section 4. Monthly rates already account for normal closures, so there are no credits for them.
  • Weather or emergency closures are announced by text and/or email as early as possible.
  • If Teamwork is unable to provide services for 30 continuous days (60 days if the delay is outside Teamwork's control), members are entitled to the remedies Indiana law provides, including a prorated refund for unused time.

10. Holds, in-season plans, and cancellations (summary)

The full rules are in the Membership Agreement and Contract. In short:

  • Holds: up to 2 months in any 365 days, with a firm restart date. Email at least one week before the billing date (waived for injuries). Billing pauses and restarts automatically. Commitment months pause too.
  • In-season: $99 per month for 1 session per week (4 per month), for 12-month members, up to 2 months in any 365 days. No discounts apply.
  • Cancellations: email memberships@teamworkbloomington.com at least 30 days before the next billing date. Memberships never cancel automatically, even after a commitment is finished.
  • Early cancellation of a commitment: an early termination fee applies, calculated as described in the Membership Agreement.

12. Athlete data and privacy

Teamwork stores account, attendance, billing, and performance data in its business systems (such as ZenPlanner, TeamBuildr, Hawkin Dynamics, and its messaging system) to coach athletes and run the business. Teamwork does not sell personal information. Performance results are only shared publicly if allowed by your Photo, Video, Name, and Likeness Permission Form.

13. Personal property

Teamwork is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged personal items. Leave valuables at home or with a parent.

14. Changes to these policies

Teamwork may update this User Agreement. Teamwork will email the account holder at least 30 days before any change takes effect. Policy changes do not raise the monthly rate locked in by a current commitment.
