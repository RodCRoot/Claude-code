Teamwork Bloomington

Photo, Video, Name, and Likeness Permission Form

Business: T2 Indiana, Inc., doing business as Teamwork Bloomington and Teamwork Indiana ("Teamwork"), 2013 S. Yost Ave, Bloomington, IN. Email memberships@teamworkbloomington.com.

Athlete: the athlete named on the Teamwork (ZenPlanner) account for which this form is signed.

Signer: the athlete's parent or legal guardian if the athlete is under 18, or the athlete if 18 or older.

Teamwork shares training clips, testing results, and athlete wins to celebrate our athletes and show families what we do. This form lets you choose what we can share. Saying no does not affect your membership in any way.

1. Choose your permission level (check one)

Your choice is recorded on this form or in the "Media Permission" question on your Teamwork registration. If no choice is recorded, Teamwork will use Limited permission until you tell us otherwise by email.

☐ Full permission. Teamwork may use the athlete's photos, video, voice, first and last name, sport, school, graduation year, and performance results as described in this form.

☐ Limited permission. Teamwork may use the athlete's photos and video, but only with the first name and last initial. No school, graduation year, or individual performance numbers.

☐ Group only. The athlete may appear in group or background shots, but Teamwork will not feature the athlete individually and will not use the athlete's name.

☐ No permission. Teamwork will not knowingly post or publish the athlete's image, voice, or name. If the athlete appears in the background of a group shot, Teamwork will blur or remove the athlete when asked.

2. What this permission covers

If you choose Full or Limited permission, you allow Teamwork to record, use, edit, and publish:

  • Photos, video, and audio of the athlete taken during training, testing, camps, and Teamwork events.
  • Performance results and progress, such as vertical jump, force plate, sprint, and strength numbers, and before-and-after comparisons.
  • Quotes, testimonials, and comments the athlete or signer gives Teamwork.
  • Athlete achievements Teamwork learns about, such as awards, records, and college commitments.

Teamwork may use this content on its social media accounts (including Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube), its website, email and text newsletters, printed materials, signs, and paid advertising for Teamwork's own services. Teamwork may edit, crop, caption, and combine content with other material, but will not edit it in a way that is false or that embarrasses the athlete.

3. Safety rules Teamwork follows

  • Teamwork will never publish the athlete's home address, phone number, email, or birth date.
  • Teamwork will not post the athlete's real-time location or training schedule.
  • Teamwork will not tag the athlete's personal social media account without permission.
  • Teamwork will not sell or license the athlete's image or name to any other business.

4. No payment, and not a NIL deal

  • Teamwork does not pay for this permission, and the signer is not owed any fee or royalty.
  • This form is not an endorsement deal or a name, image, and likeness (NIL) agreement. The athlete is not promoting a product, and Teamwork is not compensating the athlete.
  • Teamwork owns the photos and videos it creates. Content stays Teamwork's property.
  • If the athlete separately signs a paid promotional agreement with Teamwork in the future, that will be a different written agreement.

5. Changing your mind

You can change or cancel this permission at any time by email to memberships@teamworkbloomington.com. Within 10 business days of your email, Teamwork will stop new uses and remove existing posts it controls on its own accounts and website. Teamwork cannot recall printed materials already distributed, content shared or reposted by others, or paid ads already running, but will not renew them.

6. Release

To the extent allowed by law, the signer releases Teamwork from claims for invasion of privacy, right of publicity, defamation, or copyright relating to uses allowed by this form. This release does not cover any use that goes against the permission level selected above.

7. Other terms

This permission continues after the athlete's membership ends unless the signer cancels it as described in Section 5. Indiana law governs this form. An electronic signature has the same effect as a handwritten signature.
