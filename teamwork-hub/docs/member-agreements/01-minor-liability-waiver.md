### 2. Activities Covered

This document covers every activity the Participant takes part in with Teamwork, now and in the future, at 2013 S. Yost Ave or any other location Teamwork uses. That includes:

- Athletic assessments and performance testing, including force plate jumps, vertical jump testing, sprint and timing-gate testing, agility testing, and strength testing.

### 3. Known Risks (Assumption of Risk)

Athletic training is physically demanding and has real risks. Injuries can happen even when coaches are qualified, equipment is in good condition, and everyone follows the rules. The risks include, but are not limited to:

- Muscle strains, tendon and ligament sprains or tears (including ACL and Achilles injuries), joint dislocations, and fractures.
- Head, neck, and back injuries, including concussion.
- Falls, slips, trips, collisions with other athletes, walls, or equipment, and being struck by dropped or moving equipment.
- Equipment that breaks, fails, or does not prevent injury.
- Overexertion, dizziness, fainting, dehydration, heat illness, and in rare cases heart attack, cardiac arrest, stroke, or death.
- Contagious illness spread by contact with other people or shared equipment.
- Injuries caused by the actions of other participants.
- **Injuries caused by the ordinary negligence of the Released Parties**, including mistakes in instruction, supervision, programming, spotting, testing, equipment setup, equipment maintenance, or facility upkeep.

I have read this list. I understand these risks. On behalf of myself and the Participant, **I knowingly and voluntarily accept all of these risks, including risks caused by the negligence of the Released Parties.**

### 4. Release and Waiver of Claims

In exchange for Teamwork allowing the Participant to train, I, for myself, for the Participant, and for our heirs, family members, and personal representatives, **RELEASE, WAIVE, AND DISCHARGE the Released Parties from any and all claims, demands, lawsuits, and liability of any kind for personal injury, illness, death, property damage, or loss, INCLUDING CLAIMS CAUSED BY THE NEGLIGENCE OF THE RELEASED PARTIES**, arising out of or related to the Participant's activities with Teamwork.

This release includes my own claims as a parent, such as claims for medical expenses, lost wages, and loss of the Participant's services or companionship.

This release does not apply to injuries caused by gross negligence or intentional or willful misconduct, to the extent Indiana law does not allow those claims to be released.

### 5. Promise Not to Sue

I agree that neither I nor the Participant, nor anyone acting for either of us, will sue the Released Parties for any claim released in Section 4.

### 6. Parent Indemnity

If the Participant, or anyone acting for the Participant, brings a claim against any Released Party that this document releases, I agree to **indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Released Parties** from that claim, including reasonable attorney fees and costs, to the fullest extent Indiana law allows.

### 7. Health Disclosure and Fitness to Participate

- I confirm the Participant is physically able to take part in athletic training. If I am unsure, I will get a doctor's clearance before the Participant trains.
- I have told Teamwork, in writing, about every medical condition, injury, surgery, allergy, medication, or restriction that could affect the Participant's training, including asthma, diabetes, heart conditions, seizure history, and any past concussion.
- I will tell Teamwork in writing right away if anything changes, including a new injury, a concussion diagnosis, or a doctor's restriction.
- The Participant will not train with symptoms of a concussion or contagious illness, and will not return after a concussion until cleared in writing by a licensed health care provider.
- Coaches may limit, modify, or stop the Participant's training at any time for safety.

### 8. Emergency Medical Authorization

If the Participant is injured or becomes ill and I cannot be reached right away, I authorize Teamwork staff to call 911, give basic first aid and CPR, use an AED, and arrange for emergency medical transport and treatment that a licensed medical provider believes is needed. **I am responsible for all medical, ambulance, and hospital costs.** Teamwork does not provide medical insurance for participants.
