## Pre-hire checklist
- [ ] Accountability chart current
- [ ] 4 Rs written for open position
- [ ] Pay rate + guaranteed hours set
- [ ] Careers page live with offer
- [ ] Posted: Indeed / network / ads / boards

## Applicant text
> Thanks for applying to Teamwork! I have [time A] or [time B] open for a quick phone interview — which works better?

## Phone screen questions
1. Why do you want this job?
2. What's your story — how did you get into this?
3. Can you commit to [schedule]? Where are you commuting from?

## 5:15 Form (rotate questions)
1. How was your week (1–10) and why?
2. Did you complete A / B / C?
3. Your numbers: sessions coached · utilization % · check-ins · responses
4. What's on deck next week?
5. What support do you need?

## Roadmap Session prompts
- How's life outside the gym?
- What do you enjoy most / least right now?
- Where do you want to be in 6 months? 2 years?
- How can we support you more?
