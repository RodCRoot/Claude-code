# Hiring — Accountability Chart & 4 Rs
Back to [[00 Team Building MOC]]

> Design the role **before** you find the person. Biggest industry mistake: hire someone, then "fudge" a role around them.
> Sports analogy: you don't sign "a guy" — you go get a **wide receiver** or a **running back**.

## Step 1 — Accountability Chart
- One visual sheet defining the **positions** on the team (not people).
- Can be 3 circles: **Head Coach · Coach · Admin** (Doug's: Support Coach, Head Coach, GM, Admin).

## Step 2 — The 4 Rs (per position)
| R | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Role** | The title | Coach |
| **Responsibilities** | What they do, on + off floor | Coach sessions, client check-ins ("PPRs" — personal & positive reachouts), off-floor culture tasks |
| **Results** | Measurables | 10–15 sessions/wk · 25 check-ins/wk · **response rate** on check-ins |
| **Requirements** | Unteachable traits | High attention to detail · great communicator |

- Replaced KRAs/KPIs because the 4 Rs are **easier for staff to remember**.
- Why it matters: most people problems = **expectations drift** ("you started giving them more stuff to do").
- Measure **outcomes, not box-checks** — reachouts nobody answers don't count, hence response rate.
- Requirements ≠ certs or years of experience (those are teachable). Requirements = **non-negotiables you can't teach**. Know your own pet peeves and hire against them.
- Phrase it at hire: *"Here's your role, your responsibilities, and the results you need to produce."*
