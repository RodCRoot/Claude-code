# Onboarding — The Certification Model
Back to [[00 Team Building MOC]]

> Onboarding = a certification: **modules → demonstrate → certified.**

- **6 weeks**, one **theme per week** (videos, reading, practicals).
- Every week ends with a **quiz or practical — pass/fail**. Info-dumping isn't proof; demonstration is.
- **Clear finish line** — "you're certified," then development continues.
- Should never exceed **2–3 months**. Longer = bad hire or bad onboarding.
- Build modules from material you already have — **use AI** to structure them.

## The 10% Rule
- **10% of a new hire's paid hours = off-floor onboarding.** 20 hrs → 2 hrs; 40 hrs → 4 hrs.
- Budget for it. Don't stack new people on the floor 24/7 — they need **practice time**, not just games.

## Sample 6-week outline (adapt)
1. Culture & standards (5 Ps, Show–Tell–Do)
2. Programming philosophy
3. Coaching cues & movement standards
4. Injuries, progressions & regressions
5. Client communication & check-ins
6. Systems, software & admin → **Certification**
