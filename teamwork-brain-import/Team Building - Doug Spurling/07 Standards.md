# Standards
Back to [[00 Team Building MOC]]

> **Anything you let go, you've just said is the standard.**
> **Your weakest team member *is* your standard** — by paying them and letting them show up, you're saying it's OK.

## Examples
- **Punctual (5 Ps):** New coach Sammy arrives 8:01 for an 8:00 start on day one. No firing, no drama: *"One of our standards is we're on time. If something comes up, let us know."* He became a great team member.
- **Show–Tell–Do:** Show the exercise → give verbal cues → watch them do it. "Grab the straps over there" isn't it — and if you don't call it out, you've approved it.

## The loop
1. See it
2. Name it **calmly and immediately**
3. **Praise** loudly when it's right
4. Repeat forever
