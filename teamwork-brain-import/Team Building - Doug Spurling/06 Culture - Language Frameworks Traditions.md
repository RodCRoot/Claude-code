# Culture — Language, Frameworks, Traditions
Back to [[00 Team Building MOC]]

> Culture is always changing, and it's ~90% **communication**. Scaling struggles = poor communication. Repeat it until it's "brainwashing."

## L — Language
- Inside words only your team understands ("buzzer beater," "athlete analysis," "milestone moments").
- Like a family's cultural symbols — meaningful inside, confusing outside. That's the point.

## F — Frameworks
- Anything you teach over and over → put it in a **framework/acronym**.
- Doug's **5 Ps** for great coaching: Prepared · Present · Punctual · …(+2). Everyone on his team knows them because they're discussed weekly.

## T — Traditions
- Things that repeat every year so **veterans anticipate** and **newbies ask "what's that?"**
- Doug's: **100 Workout Challenge** (launches July 1) · December **toy run** · July **party**. Helps retention.

## The 6 Human Needs (Tony Robbins) — the soft side
Ask: *which need does this team member need from me right now?*
| Need | At work |
|---|---|
| Certainty | Stable hours, clear expectations |
| Variety | Not the same thing every day |
| Connection | At least one friend at work |
| Significance | What's uniquely theirs (e.g. coach who built a charity podcast) |
| Growth | Getting better at the job, new skills |
| Contribution | Giving back to clients/community |
- Applies to **clients** too.
