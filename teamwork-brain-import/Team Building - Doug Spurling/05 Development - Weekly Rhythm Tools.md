# Development — Rhythm Tools
Back to [[00 Team Building MOC]]

> **Put every one of these on the calendar** — or it won't happen.

## 1. Weekly Team Meeting = Practice
- Non-negotiable. **If it can be an email, it's an email.**
- Every week a different practice scenario: an injury case, coaching an exercise from upcoming programming so everyone cues it the same.

## 2. The 5:15 (weekly self-report)
- Google Form, bookmarked, filled out **every Friday** before last shift. Quick check-in, not a brain dump.
- 5 rotating questions: How was your week? Did you get A/B/C done? What's on deck next week? Need support? + metrics.
- **Self-reporting = accountability.** Coaches report their own **session utilization rate** (8 of 10 spots = 80%) and check-in counts. Low utilization with active clients = long-term coach problem.
- Optional: 15-min Monday 1:1 for full-timers.

## 3. Peer-to-Peer
- Coaches take another coach's session **as a participant**, **once a month**, scheduled in advance (no ambush).
- Nothing on the floor; afterward a **form to that coach + head coach**: standards hit? interaction with clients?
- Builds **peer-level** high standards, not just top-down.

## 4. Roadmap Sessions (every 6 months)
- Coffee/lunch. Life? Support? Happy in role? Head coach path? Leaving the industry?
- **Not** a strengths/weaknesses review (that's weekly). Early warning vs. the dreaded "hey, can we talk?"

## 5. Friday Owner Memo
- Weekly video or written note to the whole team: week recap, next week's focus, shout-outs.
- The **cadence** matters more than the content.
