# Hiring — Pay & Offer
Back to [[00 Team Building MOC]]

## Pay
- **Hourly shift pay** at market rate for coaches. Simplest and fairest.
- No pay-per-head / per-session-size — coaches **don't control who walks in the door** ("like paying McDonald's per burger flipped").
- Coaches exist to **service the model and current clients**; the owner's job is growth.
- Commission/percentage only for **leadership** (GM). Otherwise it kills margins and causes frustration.

## Hiring = Marketing (365 days)
- Build a **compelling offer** like a sales page.
- Doug's selling points: **stability / guaranteed hours**, set shift & pay, fun environment (sweatpants & hoodie), **small business, not corporate**.
- Contrast with the industry norm: commission, find-your-own-clients, splits → stressful and unstable.
- Put it on a **careers landing page that's always live** with a simple form.

## Distribute
- Indeed (still most popular) · paid ads → offer page · **your network (biggest)** · local job boards.
